The Small Business Website Checklist (2026)

By Billy Digitals · August 2026 · 5 min read

Building or refreshing your small business website? Use this checklist to make sure it actually earns its keep — not just looks nice.

The essentials

  • ✓ A clear headline that says what you do and who for
  • ✓ An obvious call to action on every page (call, WhatsApp, quote)
  • ✓ Fast loading — under two seconds
  • ✓ Flawless on mobile, tablet and desktop
  • ✓ Your services explained simply, with real benefits
  • ✓ Trust signals: reviews, photos of real work, guarantees

To get found on Google

  • ✓ Proper page titles and descriptions for each page
  • ✓ A page for each main service and each area you serve
  • ✓ A linked, complete Google Business Profile
  • ✓ Structured data (schema) so Google understands your business
  • ✓ A sitemap submitted in Google Search Console

To turn visitors into customers

  • ✓ A simple enquiry form that lands in your inbox instantly
  • ✓ A WhatsApp or click-to-call button on mobile
  • ✓ Clear pricing or an easy path to a quote
  • ✓ Fast, friendly follow-up when enquiries come in

The foundations

  • ✓ Secure hosting with SSL and daily backups
  • ✓ An easy way to make updates
  • ✓ Someone to keep it running (a care plan)
