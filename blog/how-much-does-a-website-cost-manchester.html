How Much Does a Website Cost in Manchester? (2026)

By Billy Digitals · August 2026 · 5 min read

If you run a business in Manchester and you're weighing up a new website, the first thing you want is a number. The honest answer is that it depends on what the site needs to do — but this guide gives you real ranges and the factors that move them, so you can budget with confidence.

The quick version

A simple, well-built brochure site for a Manchester small business is a modest one-off investment. A larger site with custom design, service pages and booking or e-commerce is more. A full custom web app is a bigger project again. Rather than a fixed price list, a good studio quotes each job to your goals so you only pay for what you actually need.

What moves the price

Pages and content

A five-page site is far quicker to design, build and test than a twenty-page site with a blog, service pages and location pages. If you need copywriting and photography direction as well as build, that adds scope — but good words and images pay for themselves.

Custom design vs a template

A template looks cheap up front and looks like everyone else. A bespoke design built around your brand costs more, loads faster, ranks better and actually converts visitors into enquiries. In a competitive city like Manchester, standing out matters.

Features

Contact forms are simple. Online booking, payments, customer logins, e-commerce or an AI assistant each add development time — and each can earn its keep by saving you hours or winning more orders.

Ongoing costs

Budget for hosting to keep the site online and secure, and optionally a care plan for updates and SEO. Both are affordable monthly options sized to your site.

Why cheap builders cost more in the end

DIY builders look tempting, but they eat your time, box you in, and rarely rank on Google. A professional build is a one-off investment that works harder for longer and pays for itself when it brings you customers.
