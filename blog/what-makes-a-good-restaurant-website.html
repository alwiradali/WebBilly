What Makes a Good Restaurant Website (That Wins Orders)

By Billy Digitals · August 2026 · 5 min read

A restaurant website has one job: turn a hungry visitor into a booking or an order. Pretty isn't enough — it has to be fast, clear and built for phones. Here's what actually works.

Put the menu front and centre

The number one thing people want is the menu — as real text, not a slow PDF or a photo they have to pinch to read. A clean, searchable menu that loads instantly keeps people on the page instead of bouncing to a competitor.

Make ordering and booking one tap away

Whether it's online ordering, a table booking, or a WhatsApp order button, the action should be obvious and instant from every page. Every extra tap loses customers.

Design for the phone first

Most food searches happen on mobile, often when someone's already hungry and deciding now. If your site is slow or fiddly on a phone, you've lost the order before they've read a word.

Show the food

Great photography sells. Real, appetising images of your actual dishes build trust and desire far more than stock photos ever will.

Get the basics unmissable

Opening hours, location with a map, phone number and delivery areas should be findable in seconds. Missing or buried details are a top reason people give up.

Be found locally

Local SEO and a strong Google Business Profile put you in front of people searching "food near me". Pair that with a fast site and you capture demand at the exact moment it happens.
