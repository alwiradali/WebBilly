Policies & guidelines

Deposit policy

A 20% deposit is required at the time of booking to secure your appointment. The deposit will be applied toward the total cost of your appointment.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours’ notice may be subject to a $50 cancellation fee + the forfeit of the deposit.

No-shows

Missed appointments without notice will be considered a no-show and may be subject to a $50 fee. Future appointments may require payment in full at the time of booking.

Refunds & concerns

Completed services are non-refundable. If Lunera Ora cancels an appointment or is unable to provide the booked service, any payment made will be fully refunded. If you have concerns regarding your service, please contact us within 48 hours so we can review the concern and determine an appropriate resolution.

Photo / video consent

Photos and/or videos may be taken during or after your appointment for treatment records. With your consent, they may also be used by Lunera Ora for social media and website content.
