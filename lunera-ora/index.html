Whitening care guide

The first 24 – 48 hours

A brighter smile is beautiful. A healthy smile comes first. Here’s how to look after yours once we’ve packed up.

Choose lighter-coloured foods & drinks

  • Water
  • Milk
  • Rice
  • Chicken
  • Eggs
  • Yogurt
  • Potatoes
  • Bananas

Avoid staining foods & drinks

  • Coffee
  • Tea
  • Red wine
  • Cola
  • Berries
  • Tomato sauce
  • Curry
  • Soy sauce
  • Other deeply coloured foods

For sensitivity

Sensitivity can be temporary. Avoid very hot or cold foods and drinks, and use toothpaste for sensitive teeth if needed.

Maintain your results

Brush twice daily, floss daily, drink plenty of water, and limit staining foods and drinks.

When to call your dentist

If you experience severe or persistent pain, gum irritation, swelling, or other concerning symptoms, contact your dentist.

FAQs

Your whitening questions, answered

Answering some of the most popular questions about whitening. Something else on your mind? Ask me.

Does teeth whitening damage teeth?

The short answer… not necessarily. When teeth whitening is performed appropriately and the product is used as directed, whitening works by interacting with stains within the tooth structure rather than simply “scrubbing” away your enamel. But not every whitening treatment is the same.

Will I experience sensitivity?

Sensitivity can happen. Some people may experience temporary sensitivity or gum irritation after whitening. This can be influenced by:

  • The whitening product
  • Concentration and application
  • Treatment duration
  • Existing tooth sensitivity
  • The condition of your teeth and gums

More whitening ≠ better whitening.

Why does professional guidance matter?

Your smile isn’t one-size-fits-all. Before whitening, it’s important to consider factors such as:

  • Existing sensitivity
  • Cavities or dental concerns
  • Gum health
  • Existing restorations
  • Your current shade and whitening goals

A professional assessment helps determine whether whitening is appropriate for you.

Who will be doing my whitening?

Ysabel — a Registered Dental Hygienist and the founder of Lunera Ora. Every visit begins with a consultation, a shade assessment and a sensitivity screening.

Which experience is right for me?

The Aura suits mild staining, The Radiance mild to moderate staining, and The Lumina moderate to severe staining. The best approach for you is confirmed at your consultation and shade assessment.

Where do you travel to?

Lunera Ora is based in Beaumont and comes to you across Beaumont, Leduc and Edmonton. Travel within 10 km is complimentary; beyond that a small fee applies by distance, and for 50+ km please inquire. Estimate your travel fee.

How long does an appointment take?

Please allow your treatment time + 15 minutes for your consultation, smile assessment, treatment preparation and personalized aftercare. The Aura and The Radiance are 1 hour; The Lumina is a 1.5-hour session plus a 45-minute follow-up appointment.

How do I pay?

A 20% deposit is paid online when you book. The balance can be paid by debit, credit, e-transfer or cash — if paying cash, please bring the exact total including your travel fee.
