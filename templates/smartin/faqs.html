Before you enquire

The questions parents actually ask

Straight answers about how sessions work, what is covered, and how to start. If yours is not here, just ask — Rod answers his own messages.

How do we get started?

Send an enquiry through the website or email Rod directly. Let him know the year group, exam board (if known), and the main challenge. He’ll suggest the best approach, explain availability and costs, and arrange a free, no-obligation chat to see if it’s the right fit.

Is it too late to start?

Usually not. Be honest with Rod about your current position, and he'll be honest in return. Year 10 offers the most time to improve, while starting in Year 11 means focusing on exam technique and high-value topics. The good news: most students are closer to success than they think.

Do you offer a trial session?

Start with a free, no-obligation chat. It gives Rod a clear picture of the situation and lets you see whether your child feels comfortable working with him. The right fit matters, as students learn best when they feel confident admitting what they don't understand.

Are sessions online or in person?

Rod offers both in-person and online tutoring. He's based in Leeds and has limited availability for local face-to-face sessions but works online with students across England and Wales. Online lessons are fully interactive, with shared past papers, live annotations, and real-time explanations. For many families, it's simply the most practical way to maintain consistent weekly support.

What happens in a session?

Every session follows a clear structure: what we're covering and why, the science explained in a way that makes sense to the student, and practice questions to check understanding. Most sessions finish with targeted work to complete before the next lesson. That consistency helps make challenging science feel manageable

One-to-one or group tuition?

Both work well. One-to-one tuition is tailored to your child's pace, while small groups help students learn from each other's questions and build confidence. Tell Rod what you think would suit your child best, and he'll offer honest advice.

What do you need for online tuition?

Just a device with a camera and microphone, a reliable internet connection, and a quiet space. A tablet works, but a laptop is usually best. We also recommend having paper and a pen handy, as working through problems on paper helps reinforce learning and makes it easier to spot any misunderstandings.

Does my child need to have their camera on?

No. We like to start with cameras on for a quick hello, as it helps build rapport and adds a personal touch. Once the session is underway, your child is welcome to switch their camera off and focus on their work.

Which exam boards do you cover?

We support AQA, Edexcel, OCR & Eduqas. While all GCSE Science courses follow the same national curriculum, each exam board has its own question style and mark scheme. Let Rod know which board your child's school uses, and we'll tailor practice materials accordingly. That exam-specific preparation often makes a bigger difference than parents expect.

Do you cover Separate Sciences (Biology, Chemistry & Physics)?

Yes. Most teaching focuses on the GCSE Combined Science content, which forms the foundation of all three Separate Sciences. Additional sessions (Bolt Ons) and masterclasses cover the extra Triple Science topics throughout the year.

If your child has significant gaps in a specific subject, Rod may recommend a specialist Biology, Chemistry or Physics tutor instead who may have more time to focus on these areas. The priority is always making sure your child gets the right support.

What about the required practicals?

Required practicals make up around 15% of GCSE Science exam marks, so they are covered in detail. Students learn the methods, variables and science behind each practical, as well as the types of exam questions that commonly appear. Understanding how and why a practical works, rather than simply memorising it, helps students pick up valuable marks in the exam.

Is there home learning?

Usually, yes, but it's kept focused and manageable. This might be a set of questions, a past-paper task, or a topic your child brings back and explains. The goal isn't to create extra work, but to reinforce learning between sessions, where much of the progress happens.

If your child has a particularly busy week, just let Rod know and the workload can be adjusted accordingly.

How will I know it's working?

You'll often notice the difference before it appears in school reports. A good sign is when your child can confidently explain a topic they previously struggled with. Rod provides honest feedback on what's improving, what still needs work, and where to focus next, so you'll always know how your child is progressing and whether the tuition is making a difference.

How much does the tutoring cost?

Tutoring fees depend on the type of support you need, whether that's one-to-one sessions or small group tuition. Get in touch with a few details and Rod will provide a clear, no-obligation quote tailored to your child's needs.

What if my child is ill or misses a session?

If your child misses a session, they can catch up by watching the Zoom recording, which is shared in Google Classroom for all students in the group. After completing the recording and any follow-up work, Rod will check that they understand the content. If extra support is needed, a short catch-up session can be arranged at the weekend.

Do you teach outside Leeds?

Yes. In-person tutoring is available across the Leeds area, while online sessions are available throughout the UK and follow the same high-quality approach as face-to-face teaching.

If you're based overseas and following the English National Curriculum, you're welcome to get in touch. Limited online slots are available on Mondays and Tuesdays for international students.

STEM Workshops and Clubs

What is STEM and why is it important?

STEM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics. Rather than treating these subjects separately, STEM helps children see how they work together to solve real-world problems.

Through hands-on activities and practical challenges, STEM develops key skills such as problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity and innovation, helping young people build confidence and prepare for future careers and opportunities

What are the STEM workshops?

Rod runs hands-on STEM workshops for schools, classes and community groups. These one-off practical sessions are designed to spark curiosity, build confidence and bring science to life. Workshops are linked to the annual British Science Week theme, with full details available on the Workshops/Clubs page.

What are the STEM after-school clubs?

Rod runs hands-on STEM after-school clubs for primary schools, available in blocks throughout the academic year (on a Monday or Tuesday). These engaging sessions encourage curiosity, creativity and problem-solving through practical science, technology, engineering and maths activities. Full details can be found on the Workshops/Clubs page.

What are the STEM holiday clubs?

Rod runs hands-on STEM holiday clubs across the Leeds area during the school holidays. Designed to be fun, engaging and curiosity-driven, the activities encourage children to explore science, technology, engineering and maths through practical challenges and experiments. For upcoming dates and details, visit the Workshop/Clubs page.

Can our school or group book a STEM session?

Absolutely! Schools, PTAs, Scouts, Guides, home-education groups and community organisations are welcome to book. Just let us know the number and ages of the children, your location, and what you're looking for. Rod will then get back to you with availability, session options and pricing.

How much does a STEM workshop cost?

Workshop costs vary depending on the number of children, session length, level of support needed and location. Get in touch with a few details, and Rod will provide a clear, no-obligation quote tailored to your group.

Practical Questions

Is Rod DBS checked?

Yes. Rod holds an Enhanced DBS check and is an experienced teacher with over 20 years' experience working in UK schools.

Parents are always welcome to ask about his current DBS status and safeguarding arrangements when making an enquiry. It's a sensible question, and Rod is happy to discuss it.

What if we need to cancel or rearrange?

Plans can change, and that's understandable. If you need to cancel or reschedule, just let Rod know as soon as possible and he'll do his best to accommodate. Cancellation and rearrangement arrangements will be clearly explained when you book, so there are no surprises.
