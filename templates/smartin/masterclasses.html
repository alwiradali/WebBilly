What they will cover

The content that sits beyond Combined Science

Every student in the country covers the Combined Science content. Students sitting the separate sciences cover roughly forty extra guided learning hours in each subject on top of it — and that extra content is what these sessions are for.

Being straight with you

Dates are not set yet

Masterclasses are scheduled through the year rather than run to a fixed timetable, and the dates depend on which topics people actually want. Rather than publish a calendar and then change it, Rod would rather hear from you first.

Tell him which subject and which topic your child is finding hardest. When enough students want the same session, it runs — and you will hear about it before anyone else.
