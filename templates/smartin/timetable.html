Four-week blocks · Y9–11

How the four weeks run

Each block runs over four weeks, one science a week, with exam technique last. Every week is self-contained but builds on the one before it.

The block

Four weeks, one job each

Week 01

Biology

The topics students most often confuse, organised into clear, memorable comparisons.

Week 02

Chemistry

The periodic table’s key knowledge and rules, where marks most often slip away.

Week 03

Physics

Choosing the correct equation, identifying its subject, and rearranging it accurately.

Week 04

Exam technique

The session that improves grades fastest, because so many marks are lost to too little exam practice.

Dates and times

Confirmed when you enquire

Start dates, session times and group sizes are set block by block, so that they fit the students who are actually booked on. Send an enquiry and Rod will come back to you personally with the next start date, the times and the current rates.

Separate science bolt-ons and weekend masterclasses are scheduled through the year alongside the main blocks — ask about these if your child is sitting Biology, Chemistry and Physics separately.
