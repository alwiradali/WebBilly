Before you book

Two things worth knowing first

There is one National Science Curriculum in England & Wales and four exam boards examining it. Which board your child sits, and which course they are on, changes how they should revise.

📋

Which exam board?

AQA, Edexcel, OCR or Eduqas. The content is the same, but each board organises it differently and writes its papers differently — so knowing which one is vital for exam preparation.

See how the boards differ →
🎓

Combined or separate?

Most students take Combined Science and come away with two GCSEs; a smaller number take Biology, Chemistry and Physics separately. Both routes are catered for, with bolt-on sessions through the year.

Four Weeks. Three sciences. One confident student.

Small group classes — enough for discussion and shared learning, small enough that nobody hides at the back. A structured, pacy, genuinely enjoyable confidence boost, built and taught by a science teacher with over 20 years in the classroom.

Week 01

Biology

The topics students most often confuse, organised into clear comparisons — cells, infection, organisation, homeostasis and the required practicals.

Week 02

Chemistry

Atomic structure, bonding, moles and electrolysis — the periodic table's key rules, and balancing equations until it is automatic.

Week 03

Physics

Energy, forces, waves and electricity — choosing the right equation, rearranging it accurately and reading the data behind it.

Week 04

Exam technique

The session that improves grades fastest: command words, a structure for 6-mark questions, timed past papers and a personal revision plan.

Questions

Everything parents usually ask

Who is the tuition for?

Y9, Y10 and Y11 students sitting GCSE Science. It suits students who are capable but have lost confidence, students who have fallen behind after missing topics, and students who understand the content but keep losing marks in exams.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Sooner than most parents expect, because the first thing that shifts is not knowledge but approach — reading the command word, showing the working, structuring a longer answer. Those habits start paying back within a few sessions and they carry into every paper afterwards.

How long the whole thing runs depends on the student. Some want a short, focused block before an exam; others keep a weekly session going through Year 10 and 11. Ask and Rod will tell you honestly what he thinks yours needs.

Which exam boards do you cover?

The core content of GCSE Combined Science is shared across AQA, Edexcel and OCR, and that is what the course teaches. Tell me your child's board when you enquire and the practice questions and past papers used will match it.

Will I hear how my child is getting on?

Yes. Homework is marked and returned, and progress feedback comes to you — not just to your child. You'll know what improved and what still needs work.
