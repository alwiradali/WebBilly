Questions, answered.

What does full management cost?

8% of the monthly rent. That covers rent collection, tenant communication, maintenance co-ordination, inspections and day-to-day issues, with one dedicated point of contact.

How much is Tenant Find?

£295 inc. VAT: marketing, enquiries, viewings and securing a suitable tenant. You manage the tenancy yourself.

Can you just collect the rent?

Yes. Rent Collection is 5% of the monthly rent: collection, arrears chasing, monthly statements, renewals and deposit administration.

How do I switch from my current agent?

We handle the whole handover: one authority letter from you, then we collect the keys, documents and deposit registrations, and your tenants get one friendly letter about where to pay rent. The tenancy itself is unaffected.

Do you handle HMO licensing?

Yes. Our specialist HMO team helps with licence requirements, applications and renewals, selective licensing, property preparation and the works needed to reach the required standard.

Who protects the deposit and my money?

Deposits are protected with the Deposit Protection Service, and client money is protected through the Propertymark Client Money Protection scheme. We answer to The Property Ombudsman (T06217).

What safety certificates does my property need?

Typically a Gas Safety Certificate, an EICR, an EPC and compliant smoke and fire alarms. We co-ordinate the inspections, certificates and any remedial work.

How long have you been letting?

We’ve been letting and managing Manchester homes since 2018, across Manchester and Salford, from Cheetham Hill to the Quays.
