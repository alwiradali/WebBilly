What makes them eternal

Every rose is folded by hand,
petal by petal

Not fresh flowers. Every bloom is made from satin ribbon, shaped and set one petal at a time, then arranged into the bouquet you asked for. Nothing wilts, nothing browns, nothing has to be thrown away on Sunday.

  • They keep, for years

    No water, no trimming, no wilting. A dust every now and then is the whole of the upkeep.
