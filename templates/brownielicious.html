The bakery

A home kitchen in Stoke-on-Trent.

Everything is mixed, baked, finished and boxed by hand for the date you give me — in small batches, in one kitchen, by one pair of hands.

The menu

Boxes, slabs, cookies and cake pops.

Brownies, blondies and brookies come in every box size. Cookies, cake pops and individually wrapped items are priced each.

(The photographs are examples — your own pictures can go straight in their place.)

Flavours

Standard and premium.

Flavours can be mixed across a box. Standard toppings are included in the box price; premium toppings are a little extra.

Premium cookies are £3 each instead of £2. Premium brownie and blondie toppings are confirmed with your order.
