Heat Fix Mcr Limited is Mohammad Ejaz — a Gas Safe registered domestic gas engineer working out of Manchester. The person who quotes your job is the person who does it.

Who you're actually ringing

There is no office, no call centre and no sales team. You ring Mohammad, Mohammad answers, and Mohammad is the one who turns up. If he's under a floor when you call, he'll ring back — usually the same hour.

That's the whole business, on purpose. A one-engineer firm can't hide behind a script or blame a subcontractor, so the work has to be right the first time. Nearly all of it comes from someone passing the number on to a neighbour, which is the only advertising that has ever really worked in this trade.

What "Gas Safe registered" means

Gas work in the UK is legally restricted. Only an engineer on the Gas Safe Register may work on gas appliances and pipework, and every registered engineer carries an ID card showing their licence number and exactly which types of work they're qualified for.

Always check the card. Ask to see it on the doorstep — a genuine engineer will hand it over before you've finished asking. You can verify any licence number yourself on the Gas Safe Register website.

What we do

Boiler installation, repair and servicing. Unvented hot water cylinders. Gas cookers, hobs and fires. Bathrooms, showers, taps and toilets. Radiators, power flushing and the leaks nobody wants at seven in the morning. Landlord gas safety certificates for anyone letting a property.

Domestic work only — houses and flats, not commercial catering or industrial plant. Keeping to what we're properly qualified for is part of the point.
