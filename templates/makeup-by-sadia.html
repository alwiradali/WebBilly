The makeup

Three ways to be done

Every booking starts with a chat about what you like, what you don't, and where you're going.

01

Natural

Skin first. Evened out, softly sculpted, and finished so it still moves and looks like you — just on your best day.

  • Skin prep and base
  • Soft brow and lash
  • A neutral lip
02

Soft glam

The in-between everyone asks for. A little more definition, a warm blended eye and a glow that reads beautifully in photos.

  • Full base and contour
  • Blended shadow, lashes
  • Lip to match the outfit
03

Party glam

For the nights that deserve it. Sharper, brighter, longer-wearing — built to last from the first photo to the last dance.

  • Long-wear full glam
  • Cut crease or halo eye
  • Strip lashes included
