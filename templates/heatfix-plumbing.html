Plumbing in Manchester

From a dripping tap to a full bathroom.

Leaks and burst pipes

If water is coming through a ceiling, turn it off at the stopcock first and ring us second. Most leaks are a joint, a valve or a corroded section rather than the whole run, so the repair is usually smaller than it looks from underneath.

  • Burst and leaking pipework
  • Ceiling and under-floor leaks traced
  • Frozen pipes thawed and lagged
  • Stopcocks and isolation valves replaced

Taps, toilets and showers

The small jobs most people put up with for months. A dripping tap, a toilet that runs on, a shower with no pressure or a waste that keeps blocking. Usually one visit, and we carry the common parts.

  • Dripping and stiff taps
  • Toilets running on or not filling
  • Showers, pumps and low pressure
  • Blocked wastes and traps

Bathrooms and showers

Full bathroom installations, including the pipework, the waste, the tiling back and the heated towel rail. We give you a written quote with the stages in it, so you know which days you will be without a bathroom before you commit.

  • Full bathroom installations
  • Bath to shower conversions
  • Heated towel rails and radiators
  • Wet rooms and level access

Hot water and cylinders

Unvented cylinders need an engineer with the separate G3 qualification, and they need servicing yearly like a boiler does. If your hot water has gone lukewarm or the tundish is running, that is usually an expansion vessel or an immersion rather than a new cylinder.

  • Unvented cylinders installed and serviced
  • Immersion heaters and thermostats
  • Expansion vessels and tundish overflows
  • Hot water pressure and flow problems
