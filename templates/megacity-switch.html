Switch managing agent

Unhappy with your
current agent?

Switching is easier than most landlords think, and we do all of it. Your tenants stay, your tenancy stays, the rent keeps coming. You sign one letter of authority and we handle the rest.

  1. You sign one authority letter.That’s the only paperwork you touch.
  2. We contact your current agent.Keys, tenancy agreements, deposit registrations, certificates and tenant details, collected for you.
  3. Your tenants get one friendly letter.Telling them where the rent goes now. Nothing else in their tenancy changes.

No switching fee. Full management 8% of the monthly rent.

What usually prompts it

The three reasons landlords
call us.

Nobody answers the phone

The most common one by a distance. You are chasing your own agent for an update on your own property, and the repair the tenant reported three weeks ago has not moved.

The statements do not add up

Deductions that were never explained, invoices you did not approve, or a statement you have to decode. You should be able to read one in thirty seconds.

Compliance has drifted

A gas certificate that lapsed, an EICR nobody booked, a deposit protected late. These are the ones that cost real money if a tenancy ever goes wrong.
