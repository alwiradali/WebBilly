Boilers and Radiators in Manchester

New boilers, breakdowns, servicing, and radiators that will not warm up.

New boilers

A straight combi swap in the same spot is normally one day. Moving the boiler to another wall, taking out a back boiler, or going from a tank-and-cylinder system to a combi is usually two. We tell you which yours is when we quote, not halfway through the second day.

  • Combi, system and regular boilers
  • Back boiler removals and conversions
  • Fixed written quote before anything starts
  • Manufacturer warranty registered for you

Breakdowns and repairs

No heating, no hot water, a fault code on the display or a boiler that keeps dropping pressure. We diagnose it on the first visit and tell you what it needs and what it costs before we start, so you can decide whether it is worth repairing or replacing.

  • No heating or no hot water
  • Fault codes and lockouts
  • Pressure dropping and needing topping up
  • Leaks, noises and pump failures

Annual servicing

A service is where a blocked condensate, a sooting burner or a tired seal gets caught before it strands you in January. Nearly every manufacturer voids the warranty without a service recorded every twelve months, and it is the check that keeps combustion clean, which is what keeps carbon monoxide out of the room.

  • Full combustion analysis
  • Seals, burner and heat exchanger checked
  • Condensate and flue inspected
  • Written service record for your warranty

Radiators and cylinders

Cold at the bottom and warm at the top is sludge settling along the base, and it needs a flush. Cold at the top and warm at the bottom is just air, and a bleed key fixes it in a minute. We will tell you which one you have got rather than selling you the expensive answer.

  • Cold radiators diagnosed and flushed
  • Powerflushing and system cleaning
  • New radiators, valves and thermostatic heads
  • Unvented cylinders installed and serviced
