What we need from you

Every applicant is referenced the same way, and we will tell you honestly before a viewing whether you are likely to pass.

  • Proof of income that comfortably covers the rent, or a UK-based guarantor
  • Photo ID for the Right to Rent check, which is a legal requirement
  • A previous landlord reference where you have one
  • Credit check and confirmation of employment or student status

Under the Tenant Fees Act we cannot charge you for referencing, for the tenancy agreement or for an inventory. The deposit is capped at five weeks' rent and is protected with the Deposit Protection Service within 30 days. More on renting with us →
