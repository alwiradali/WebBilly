Our philosophy

A cake as a
work of art

Inspired by the elegance of life's most cherished moments, Krem&Choc cakes are designed to evoke emotions, stir memories, and elevate every occasion.

Founded by Zahra — a sociologist with a deep understanding of human connections and cultural celebrations — we approach each cake as a work of art, reflecting your personal story, values, and the relationships that matter most to you.

We believe a cake should not only taste divine, but become the centrepiece of your celebration: a true reflection of your individuality.
