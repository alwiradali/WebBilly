The Ascension Course · a six-month journey

Seven centres.
One ascension.

01 Root — Muladhara

We begin at the ground. Safety, belonging and the quiet strength of being held — by the earth, and by the circle.

02 Sacral — Svadhisthana

Creativity and feeling return to the body. Movement gets softer, breath gets braver.

03 Solar Plexus — Manipura

Your fire. Boundaries, confidence and the warmth of a woman who knows her own worth.

04 Heart — Anahata

The turning point. Grief moves, gratitude lands, and the chest learns to stay open.

05 Throat — Vishuddha

Your voice, spoken and sung. Truth said out loud in a room that wants to hear it.

06 Third Eye — Ajna

Intuition sharpens. You start trusting the knowing that was always there.

07 Crown — Sahasrara

Connection to something greater. You leave lighter than you came — and you keep rising.
