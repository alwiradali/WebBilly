Terms of use

The rules for using this website, and how quotes, bookings and payment work.

Last updated: August 2026

1. About these terms

This website is operated by HeatFix Mcr Limited, a heating and plumbing business based in Manchester. By using the site you accept these terms. If you don't accept them, please don't use the site.

2. The information here

We keep this site accurate and up to date, but it's general information about our services — not technical or legal advice for your particular property. Anything about regulations, warranties or safety is a plain-English summary; the official rules and the manufacturer's own documentation always take precedence. Never act on a page here in place of getting an appliance looked at.

This site does not publish prices. The price for your job is the one we give you in writing after seeing it, and nothing said in general terms on these pages is a quote.

3. Quotes

  • Quoting is free and there is no call-out charge for coming to look.
  • A written quote is valid for 30 days unless it says otherwise.
  • A quote covers the work described in it. If something is only discovered once panels or floorboards come up — a hidden leak, corroded pipework, an unsafe existing installation — we will stop, tell you what it means for the price, and wait for you to agree before carrying on.
  • Where a job can't sensibly be priced in advance, we'll say so and agree an hourly rate with you first.

4. Bookings

  • Booking a slot online reserves that window. It is confirmed once we've spoken to you by phone.
  • Slots are two-hour arrival windows, not fixed appointment times. We'll text when we set off.
  • Emergencies can move a planned visit. If that happens we will ring you as early as we can and rebook at your convenience.
  • Please cancel or move a booking with as much notice as you can. If nobody is at the property when we arrive for a confirmed visit, we may charge for the wasted journey.

5. The work

  • All gas work is carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
  • We work on domestic property only.
  • If we find an appliance or installation that is immediately dangerous, we are required to make it safe and, where appropriate, disconnect it and report it. We cannot leave a dangerous appliance in use, whatever you'd prefer.
  • We need reasonable access, a supply of water and electricity, and somewhere to park.
  • HeatFix Mcr Limited does not provide its own guarantee or warranty on parts or appliances. Any warranty is the manufacturer's, on their terms — explained here. This does not affect your statutory rights, including the right to work carried out with reasonable care and skill under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

6. Payment

  • Payment is due on completion unless we've agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Bank transfer or cash. Your invoice is sent as a PDF to your phone or email.
  • On larger installations we may ask for a deposit towards materials. It will be shown on the invoice and deducted from the total.
  • Goods supplied remain our property until they are paid for in full.

7. Cancelling

Where you have agreed to work away from our business premises — which is most jobs — you generally have 14 days to cancel under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. If you have asked us in writing to start within that period, and we have, you are liable for the work done up to the point you cancel. Emergency call-outs you have specifically requested are treated as work you asked us to begin immediately.

8. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law. Beyond that, we are not liable for loss you could not reasonably have expected, or for business losses. Our liability for any one job is limited to the value of that job, except where the law says otherwise.
