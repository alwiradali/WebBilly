Flower Subscriptions

Fresh Flowers, On Repeat

Set it once and forget it. Pause, skip or cancel any time, from a link in your own email — you never have to ring anybody to stop it.

What a subscription is

A standing order for flowers. Rachel makes something to the value of your plan on the same day each month and delivers it, and you never have to remember to order.

It is not a box of stems to arrange yourself. It arrives finished, in water, the way anything else from her does.

Who it is for

People who like having flowers in the house and never get round to buying them, and people sending them to a parent who would never buy their own.

What arrives

A finished, hand-arranged bouquet, seasonal, never the same twice. A vase is included on every plan.

When

The same day each month, chosen by you. Rachel texts the morning she is coming.

Stopping it

One link in your email. Pause for a month, skip one, or cancel outright — no notice period and no phone call.

Questions

Before you subscribe

Can I send it to someone else?

Yes, and most people do. Put their address in and the card message in the notes. Rachel can write a different message each month if you send her the list.

What if I am away?

Skip that month from the link in your email. You are not charged for a skipped month and it does not shorten the subscription.

Do I get the same flowers every time?

No, and that is deliberate. She buys what is best that week, so a January delivery looks nothing like a June one. If there is something you never want in it — lilies, say, or anything scented — tell her once and she remembers.

When am I charged?

On the same date each month, starting the day the first bouquet goes out. Nothing is taken before the first delivery.
