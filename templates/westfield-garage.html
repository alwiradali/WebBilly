Before you ring

The usual questions.

Will you tell me the price before you start?

Yes — that's the whole point. The car gets looked at, then you get a call with what's wrong and what it costs. Nothing gets done until you agree to it, and if something else turns up mid-job you get another call, not a bigger bill.

Do you work on my make of car?

All makes and models. If a job genuinely needs a main-dealer tool we'll say so rather than have a go at it.

The engine light is on. Is it safe to drive in?

A steady light usually means drive it gently and get it looked at soon. A flashing light means stop and ring us — carrying on can turn a cheap fix into an expensive one. Either way, ring and describe it.

How do I know when the timing belt is due?

It's on mileage or age, whichever comes first, and it varies a lot between engines. Tell us the car and we'll tell you the interval — it's the one job that's always cheaper than what happens if it lets go.

Can I wait while it's done?

For smaller jobs, usually. For anything that needs parts ordering in or a few hours on the ramp it's better to leave it with us. Say when you book and we'll tell you honestly which it is.

Do you keep the old parts?

If you'd like to see what came off, just ask when you drop the car in and we'll put them aside for you.
