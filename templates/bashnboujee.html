Bash'n'Boujee

Your day, dressed properly.

We take pride in our work & it shows in the room.

Bash'n'Boujee is a London event-decor and party-styling studio. We build the bit of the party everyone photographs — the balloon garland behind the cake, the hoop with the neon sign inside it, the flower arch at the door, the stage the couple sit on.

Every set-up is designed around what you send us: your colours, your theme, the size of the room. Tell us your ideas, your date and your requirements, and we will come back with a reasonable, personalised price.

How it works

Four steps, two weeks, one very good room.

Please give us at least two weeks' notice, and allow a minimum of three hours of preparation on the day itself.

01

Tell us the day

Message us with your date, your venue, your colours and how long you would like to keep the stand. Pictures of anything you like help enormously.

02

We price it

We come back with a reasonable, personalised price for exactly what you have described — no packages you have to squeeze yourself into.

03

Secure the date

A 25% deposit books the day out for you. The balance is due on the day, once it is all up.

04

We set up

We arrive, build it, and leave you a room worth photographing. Collection is arranged around the time you booked the stand for.
