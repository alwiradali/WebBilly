Forever florals

The same flowers, without the wilting

The same arranging, in faux stems instead of cut ones. It needs no water and it does not drop — which makes it the right choice for a gift that has to travel, an office, or a room that never gets enough light.

  • Arranged by hand exactly as a fresh bouquet is, so it does not read as artificial.
  • No water, no wilting — it keeps looking the way it did on the day.
  • Baskets, bouquets or a standing arrangement, whichever suits where it is going.
