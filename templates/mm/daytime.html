Weekday daytime · Registering interest

Chemistry during the school day

I'm looking at running Chemistry tuition in weekday daytime hours, not just evenings and Sundays. It is aimed at anyone whose week already has space in it — S6 pupils with free periods, anyone on a flexible or blended timetable, and families educating at home. Classes are live on Zoom, so a free period is genuinely long enough: there is no journey at either end of it.

Who it's for

Built around the people with daytime hours

Most tuition assumes a pupil is free at seven in the evening. Plenty of them are not — and plenty of them are free at eleven in the morning instead.

🕚

S6 with free periods

A free period or two in the week is enough for a proper session. Chemistry gets your attention while you are still fresh, rather than at the end of a full school day.

🗓️

Flexible timetables

Blended, part-time or college-linked weeks leave gaps that are hard to fill. A daytime slot fits into one of those gaps instead of competing with the evening.

🏡

Home educated

If your learning already happens in the daytime, Chemistry should not be the one subject pushed to the evening. SQA National 5, Higher and Advanced Higher, taught to the same course specifications.

Being shaped now

This page asks, rather than tells

Nothing fixed yet

Whether it runs one-to-one or as a small group depends on you

I have not set the format, the days or the times, and I would rather not guess at them. If a handful of people want the same level at the same hour, a small group is the better and cheaper way to teach it. If the interest is spread across levels and times, one-to-one makes more sense. Either way it should be built around the people who actually come forward.

So tell me what would suit you. The more of this you can say, the faster it takes shape:

  • The level — National 5, Higher or Advanced Higher
  • Which daytime hours you are actually free, and on which days
  • Whether you would prefer one-to-one or would happily share a small group
  • Whether this is S6 free periods, a flexible timetable, or home education
