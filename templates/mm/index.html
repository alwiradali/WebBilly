About Lynsey

A qualified teacher who knows the Scottish curriculum inside out

I've spent more than 20 years teaching Chemistry in the classroom, at every level from National 5 through to Advanced Higher. I've also marked for the SQA, so I know precisely how marks are awarded — and where pupils quietly lose them.

Alongside teaching I've been a pastoral care teacher, supporting young people through exam stress, mental health and career choices. I'm a mum myself, so I understand the highs and lows of exams from both sides.

  • Fully qualified teacher · GTCS registered
  • PVG certified — happy to share on request
  • Former SQA marker — exam technique is my speciality
  • Inclusive, patient and adaptable — including ASN & autism support
