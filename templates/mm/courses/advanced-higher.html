SQA Advanced Higher Chemistry

Advanced Higher Chemistry — university-level, a year early

Advanced Higher is closer to first-year university than to Higher. Small-group tuition with Lynsey Robinson, a qualified Chemistry teacher and SQA marker with over 20 years' experience at this level.

What makes Advanced Higher different

Advanced Higher asks for something Higher never quite does: genuine independence. Pupils are expected to reason from principles, handle unfamiliar problems without a worked template, and carry out a substantial project largely under their own steam.

The content is also more abstract. Orbitals, thermodynamics and reaction mechanisms are the point at which Chemistry stops being describable in everyday terms, and pupils who got through Higher on strong memory often find that approach stops working.

Classes at this level are as much about how to think as what to know — and with no more than eight pupils, there is room for the long discussions that this material genuinely needs.

Is this the right class?

This class suits pupils taking Advanced Higher Chemistry, particularly those heading for Medicine, Dentistry, Veterinary Medicine, Pharmacy or a Chemistry-related degree, where the grade carries real weight.

It is also the right place for a capable pupil who is finding that the study habits which carried them through Higher are no longer enough — which is extremely common at this level and not a sign of anything going wrong.

Support with the project is included. It is worth a substantial share of the final award and is the part pupils most often underestimate.
