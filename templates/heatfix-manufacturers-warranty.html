Manufacturer's warranty

The warranty comes
from the maker.

When you buy a new boiler it carries a warranty from the company that built it. Here's what that actually covers, and what keeps it valid.

Whose warranty it is

A new boiler is warranted by the manufacturer — Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal, Baxi, Glow-worm, Viessmann or whoever made it. It is a promise from them about their product, not a promise from the person who fitted it, and the terms are set entirely by them. Lengths vary a lot by make and model: some are two years, some are five, some run to ten or twelve on particular ranges.

To be clear: HeatFix Mcr Limited does not offer its own warranty or guarantee. Any warranty on a boiler, cylinder, pump or part is the one supplied by that manufacturer, on their terms. We're happy to tell you exactly what a given model comes with before you decide.

What normally keeps it valid

Manufacturers attach conditions. They differ between makes, so always read the booklet that comes with the appliance, but almost all of them expect:

  • Installation by a Gas Safe registered engineer, to the maker's instructions and current Building Regulations.
  • Registration within a set window — usually 30 days from installation. Miss it and the warranty can drop to the statutory minimum.
  • Commissioning recorded on the Benchmark checklist in the back of the installation manual, filled in and signed on the day.
  • A service every twelve months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, each one recorded in the Benchmark log. Miss a year and most manufacturers treat the warranty as void.
  • The system treated and protected — flushed properly, inhibitor added, and on many makes a magnetic filter fitted and cleaned annually.

Keep the logbook. When a manufacturer sends an engineer under warranty, the Benchmark log and the service records are the first things they ask to see.

What it usually covers — and what it doesn't

Generally coveredGenerally not covered
Parts that fail through a manufacturing defectLimescale and sludge damage
Labour to replace those partsFrozen or blocked condensate pipes
Faults inside the appliance casingAnything outside the boiler — radiators, valves, cylinders, controls
Damage from incorrect installation or no annual service
Accidental damage, misuse or third-party tampering

A warranty is not a service contract and it is not insurance. It will not send someone out because the pressure is low or a radiator is cold — those are system faults, and they're what we're for.

How we handle it

On any new boiler we fit, the Benchmark checklist is completed and signed on the day and the logbook and records are left with you. If you'd like the appliance registered with the manufacturer we'll walk you through it, or do it with you before we leave — either way it goes in your name, so the warranty is yours and moves with the house.

If a boiler we've installed develops a fault inside its warranty period, ring us first. Often we can diagnose it in a visit and tell you whether it's a warranty claim on the manufacturer or a system problem — which saves you waiting a week for an appointment that turns out to be chargeable.
