Good to know

Before you book.

Which areas do you cover?
We're based in the Manchester area and cover the surrounding North West — Stockport, Cheadle, Heald Green and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
How much does a job cost?
Every garden and patio is different, so we give a fixed price up front after seeing a photo or having a quick look. No call-out fees and no obligation — the quote is always free.
Do you take the garden waste away?
Yes. Clippings, cuttings and clearance waste are all removed and disposed of responsibly as part of the job unless you'd prefer to keep it.
Are you insured?
We're fully insured for the work we carry out, so you're completely covered while we're on site.
How quickly can you come out?
We often have availability the same week. Message us with what you need and we'll get you booked in as soon as we can.
