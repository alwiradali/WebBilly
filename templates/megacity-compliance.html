Safety certificates & compliance

Certificates,
handled.

Landlords carry a long list of safety and compliance responsibilities. We co-ordinate the inspections, certificates and any associated works your property needs, and keep track of when each one is due.

  • Gas Safety Certificates
  • Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICR)
  • Energy Performance Certificates (EPC)
  • Smoke and fire alarm requirements
  • Associated remedial and compliance works
Check my property’s compliance

What your property needs

The list, in plain English.

Gas Safety Certificate

An annual check of every gas appliance and flue by a Gas Safe registered engineer, with a copy served on the tenant. This one is not optional and not negotiable.

EICR

An Electrical Installation Condition Report at least every five years, and at the start of a new tenancy. Any C1 or C2 fault has to be remedied within 28 days.

EPC

A valid Energy Performance Certificate before the property is marketed, currently E or above to let legally. The minimum standard is under review, so it is worth knowing your rating now.

Alarms

A smoke alarm on every storey and a carbon monoxide alarm in any room with a fixed combustion appliance, tested on the first day of the tenancy.

Right to Rent

Every adult occupier’s immigration status checked and evidenced before they move in, with follow up checks where a time limited status applies.

Documents served

How to Rent guide, EPC, gas certificate and the deposit prescribed information, all served correctly and inside the deadline. Getting this wrong is what invalidates a possession notice.
