How to Order

DM the details,
she bakes the rest.

step one

Send your order

Message with the collection date, quantity, flavour(s), colour scheme or theme — and any photo inspiration you love.

step two

Confirm & pay

Orders are confirmed once full payment is received by bank transfer. Then your date is locked in.

step three

Baked fresh for you

Cakes and cupcakes need a week's notice; brownies and cookies five days. Everything is baked to order — never before.

step four

Collection day

Collect at the agreed time and take it home gorgeous. Running late? Just say — communication keeps it simple.

Good to Know

The little print,
kept sweet.

Notice periods

Cakes & cupcakes need at least 1 week's notice; brownies & cookies at least 5 days. Last-minute? Ask — she'll try.

Payment first, then baking

Orders are processed once full payment arrives by bank transfer. That's what locks your date in.

Collection

Please arrive on time — and if you're running late, send a message as soon as you can so your order's kept safe.

Handmade, honestly

Every item is handmade and matched to your inspiration as closely as possible — with the small variations that make it yours.

Allergens

Everything is made in a kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, gluten and eggs — no product can be guaranteed allergen-free.

Get it right first time

Double-check date, quantity and theme when ordering — changes have small windows (48h for biscuits, 24h for loaves & muffins).
