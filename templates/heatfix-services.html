Landlord Gas Safety Certificate

A CP12 covering every gas appliance and the pipework, issued on the same visit. If you let a property in England or Wales it has to be done every twelve months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and the tenant gets a copy.

  • Every gas appliance and the pipework
  • Issued the same visit
  • Copies for you and the tenant
  • Cookers, hobs and fires included

Gas leak detection and repair

If you can smell gas right now, stop reading and ring the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. It is free and it runs 24 hours a day. Open the doors and windows, turn the gas off at the meter if you can reach it safely, do not touch any electrical switch, and get everyone out.

They make the property safe and isolate whatever is leaking. Putting it right afterwards — tracing the escape, replacing the failed section or appliance connection and testing the installation — is the part we do.

  • Gas escapes traced on pipework and appliances
  • Tightness testing on the installation
  • Failed sections and connections replaced
  • Appliances recommissioned and left tested
