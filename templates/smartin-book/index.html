03The subjects

Three sciences.
No hiding from one.

Combined Science means nobody gets to avoid the one they find hardest. So all three are taught properly, alongside the maths and practical skills that run through every paper.

5
03Covered in full

Biology

Cells and transport, infection and response, organisation, homeostasis, inheritance, ecosystems — plus the required practicals and how they get examined.

Chemistry

Atomic structure, bonding, quantitative chemistry, chemical changes, electrolysis, rates and equilibrium. Balancing equations until it needs no thought.

Physics

Energy, forces and motion graphs, waves, electricity and magnetism — and rearranging any equation without panicking.

Exam technique

Command words, six-mark structure, timed past papers with live feedback.

Week four changes grades fastest, because most lost marks aren't lost through ignorance — they're lost through answering the wrong question, or the right one badly.

Describe ≠ explain. This one costs marks every year ✓
