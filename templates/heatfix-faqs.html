Questions

The things
everyone asks.

Straight answers, including the ones that talk you out of spending money. If yours isn't here, ring 07890 452629 and ask.

Can you come out today?

Often, yes. If you have no heating, no hot water, a gas smell or water coming through a ceiling, ring rather than use the form — the phone gets answered, the form gets read. The online booking slots are for planned work: services, quotes, installs and certificates.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Every gas job is carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and the ID card comes out on the doorstep before you have to ask. The card shows the licence number and exactly which categories of gas work the engineer is qualified for. You can check any number yourself on the Gas Safe Register.

Do you do landlord gas safety certificates (CP12)?

Yes — a CP12 landlord gas safety record covering every gas appliance and the pipework, issued on the same visit. If you let a property in England or Wales you must have this done every twelve months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, keep a copy for two years, and give the tenant a copy within 28 days (or at the start of a new tenancy).

How long does a new boiler take to fit?

A straight combi swap in the same position is normally one day. Moving the boiler to another wall, converting from a back boiler, or going from a tank-and-cylinder system to a combi is usually two. We'll tell you which yours is when we quote, not halfway through the second day.

Why does my boiler keep losing pressure?

Nearly always one of three things: a small leak on the pipework or a radiator valve, an expansion vessel inside the boiler that has lost its charge, or a pressure relief valve passing water out through the wall. Topping it up every week hides the fault rather than fixing it — and the constant fresh water brings oxygen into the system, which corrodes it from the inside. There's a longer write-up here.

My radiators are cold at the bottom. What's wrong?

Cold at the bottom and warm at the top usually means sludge — magnetite settling along the base of the radiator, which needs a flush. Cold at the top and warm at the bottom is just air, and a bleed key fixes it in a minute. Which is which, and how each one is put right.

Can you fit a gas cooker I've bought myself?

Yes. Buying the appliance yourself is completely normal — we just connect and test it. By law the connection has to be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and it includes the correct bayonet fitting, a stability chain or bracket, and a tightness test on the installation. More on the rules here.

Does my boiler really need servicing every year?

Yes, for two reasons that matter more than the rest. A service is where a blocked condensate, a sooting burner or a tired seal gets caught before it strands you in January — and nearly every manufacturer's warranty is void without a service recorded every twelve months. It is also the check that keeps combustion clean, which is what keeps carbon monoxide out of the room. More on carbon monoxide here.

Are you insured?

Yes. HeatFix Mcr Limited carries public liability insurance as well as being Gas Safe registered — the two are separate things and a decent engineer has both. Landlords, letting agents and managing agents often want to see the certificate before work starts; ask and it gets sent over.

Manchester and Stockport, and the whole belt between them — 61 areas including Salford, Eccles, Chorlton, Didsbury, Withington, Burnage, Levenshulme, Reddish, the Heatons, Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Gatley and Bramhall. Full list here. If you're a little outside that, ring and ask; the answer is usually yes.

How do I pay, and do I get an invoice?

Bank transfer or cash on completion. Your invoice arrives as a PDF on your WhatsApp or email before we leave, with the work itemised, along with any gas safety records for the visit.

I can smell gas. What should I do?

Don't ring us first. Open the doors and windows, turn the gas off at the meter if you can reach it safely, don't touch any light or electrical switch, don't smoke, and get everyone out. Then call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 from outside the property. It is free and it runs 24 hours a day. Ring us afterwards to put right whatever they isolate.
