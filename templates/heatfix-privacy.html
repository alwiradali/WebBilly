Privacy

Privacy policy

What we do with the details you give us, in plain English. Short version: we use them to quote and carry out your job, and nothing else.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

HeatFix Mcr Limited ("we", "us") is a heating and plumbing business based in Manchester. We are the data controller for the personal information described on this page. You can reach us at heatfixmcr@hotmail.com or on 07890 452629.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, phone number, email address, the address of the property and postcode, and whatever you tell us about the job — including any photographs you send.
  • Booking details. The date and time slot you choose and any access notes you add.
  • Job records. Notes, photographs of the work, invoices and any gas safety records we issue.

We do not take card details on this website and we do not run advertising trackers on it.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To quote for and carry out your job — necessary to take steps at your request before a contract, and then to perform that contract.
  • To contact you about the visit — same basis.
  • To issue invoices and keep accounting records — legal obligation.
  • To issue and retain gas safety records — legal obligation under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998.
  • To use photographs of completed work on this website — only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time. Photographs are chosen so that people, documents and anything identifying the address are not shown.

Who sees it

Only Mohammad Ejaz, plus the suppliers needed to run the business:

  • Our web host, which serves this site and receives enquiry form submissions.
  • Our email and messaging providers, where your enquiry lands.
  • Our accountant and HMRC, for invoices and tax records.
  • Gas Safe Register, where a gas safety record has to be notified.

We do not sell your details, we do not share them for marketing, and we do not pass them to lead-generation companies.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become jobs: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 6 years, as HMRC requires.
  • Gas safety records: at least 2 years, as the regulations require.
  • Job photographs: kept while they are useful, and deleted on request.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of the information we hold about you, correct anything wrong, delete it where we are not legally obliged to keep it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable format. Email heatfixmcr@hotmail.com and we'll respond within one month.

If you're not happy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The invoice tool stores your own business details in your browser's local storage, on your device only — that never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for the font file.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top of the page.
