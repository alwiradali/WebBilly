Aftercare

Keep them blooming longer.

A few simple care tips for the longest-lasting blooms.

01

Trim the stems

Before arranging, cut the stems at an angle with a sharp knife or garden shears — it helps the flowers drink water efficiently.

02

Use clean water

Fill the vase with fresh, cool water. Avoid hot water, as it can damage the flowers.

03

Choose the right spot

A cool, well-lit area, away from direct sunlight and heat sources.

04

Change the water

Replace it every 2–3 days to prevent bacteria and keep the flowers fresh.

05

Remove wilted flowers

Take out any wilting blooms and dead outer petals to prolong the life of the bouquet.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far in advance should an order be placed?

At least 5 days before the date needed. For busy dates like Valentine’s and Mother’s Day, earlier is always better.

How does the deposit work?

A 50% deposit secures the date and is non-refundable. The balance can be paid at checkout, or before collection or delivery.

Where is delivery available?

Local delivery covers Birmingham and Solihull, subject to conditions. Collection is from B92, Solihull. The postcode checker above gives a quick answer.

Can a design be changed?

Absolutely — size, colours, wrapping, ribbon and lettering can all be personalised. Inspiration pictures are always welcome.

What if a flower is out of season?

Seasonal flowers are swapped for similar blooms in the same colours, so the bouquet always looks its freshest.

Are bridal and mehndi florals available?

Yes — gajre, wrist corsages, bridal bouquets and more. Send a bridal enquiry with the date and the outfit colours for a personal quote.
