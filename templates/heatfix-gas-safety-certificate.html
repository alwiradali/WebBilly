Landlord Gas Safety Certificate (CP12)

Every appliance checked, certificate issued on the same visit.

What a CP12 is

A landlord gas safety record, still called a CP12 by nearly everyone. A Gas Safe registered engineer checks every gas appliance in the property and the pipework feeding them, and issues a dated record listing what was checked and whether each appliance passed.

  • Every gas appliance in the property
  • The pipework and the meter
  • Flues and ventilation
  • Issued and emailed on the same visit

What the law asks of you

If you let a property in England or Wales you must have a gas safety check carried out every twelve months by a Gas Safe registered engineer. You must give the tenant a copy within 28 days of the check, or at the start of a new tenancy, and keep a copy yourself for two years.

  • Every twelve months, without fail
  • Copy to the tenant within 28 days
  • Copy at the start of a new tenancy
  • Keep your own copy for two years

What happens on the visit

We test each appliance for gas tightness, check that it is burning correctly, confirm the flue is clearing properly and that the room has the ventilation the appliance needs. It usually takes under an hour for a flat with a boiler and a hob.

  • Gas tightness test on the installation
  • Combustion and burner pressure checked
  • Flue flow and spillage tested
  • Ventilation and safety devices confirmed

If something does not pass

An appliance that fails is classed and, where it is genuinely dangerous, disconnected with your permission and labelled. You get told exactly what is wrong and what it takes to put right, in writing, before any repair work is agreed. Nothing gets done on the day without you knowing the cost.

  • Clear classification of any defect
  • Written explanation of the fix
  • Repairs quoted before they are carried out
  • Re-issued certificate once it passes
