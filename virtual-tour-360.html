The process

Capture to live link in a week

Scope

A short call and a floor plan. We agree the shot list, the route through the building and what each hotspot needs to do.

Capture

One shoot day for a typical two-floor office. Early, late or weekend so nobody has to clear a desk.

Stitch

Panoramas stitched, levelled, colour-matched and retouched. Nadir patched, reflections and rigging removed.

Build

Spaces linked into a walk-through graph, floor plans drawn, hotspots written, brand applied. You review a private link.

Hand over

Live on your domain, plus the source folder, the editor and a 30-minute walkthrough so your team can edit it.

The software

Built by us, owned by you

Most tour platforms rent you a viewer and hold your panoramas hostage behind a monthly seat fee. Ours is a single JavaScript file and a JSON file. It runs on any static host, works offline on an intranet, and keeps working the day you stop paying anyone.

True photosphere viewer

Inertial drag, pinch and scroll zoom, keyboard control, and a walk-through transition that dollies you into the next space instead of cutting.

Hotspot graph

Navigate, info, media and form hotspots. Spaces link the way the building actually works, not as a slideshow.

Live floor plan

Per-floor plan with a radar cone showing exactly which way the visitor is facing. Click the plan to teleport.

Guided tour

An auto-play route for reception screens and pitch decks. It runs unattended and picks up wherever a visitor takes over.

Deep links

Every angle has its own URL. Send a link that opens on the exact corner of the exact room you're talking about.

Phone-native

Progressive loading

A preview panorama appears immediately and sharpens to full resolution behind it, so the tour opens instantly on any connection.

In-browser editor

Your team clicks to place a hotspot, aims it, labels it, links it — then exports the tour as one file. No developer needed.

Accessible & measured

Keyboard navigable, honours reduced-motion, and reports which spaces and hotspots visitors actually use.

Questions

The things people ask first

How long does a 360° capture take?

A typical 18,000 sq ft office over two floors is captured in one day. We shoot around your working hours — early mornings, evenings or weekends — so nobody has to clear a floor.

Do I own the tour?

Yes. The finished tour is a folder of static files — the viewer, the tour configuration and your panoramas. We hand it over with a perpetual licence to host it wherever you like, including your own domain or a closed intranet, for as long as you like.

Can visitors book or enquire from inside the tour?

Yes. Hotspots can open information panels, video, floor plans, downloadable packs, booking forms, or link straight into your existing room-booking system.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — it runs in the browser on phones, tablets and desktops with no app or plugin. On a phone you can also just move the handset to look around.

Can you re-shoot one room later?

Yes. Each space is a separate panorama, so a single room can be re-captured and dropped in without rebuilding the tour — useful after a refit, or to add a golden-hour version of a terrace.

What do you need from us on the day?

Access, a contact on site, and rooms tidy and lit the way you'd want them photographed. We work room by room and it's silent — no lighting rigs, no cables across the floor.

Tell us the footprint, the floors and what the tour has to do. You'll get a fixed price, a shoot date and a link to a live example of the same build — usually the same day.
