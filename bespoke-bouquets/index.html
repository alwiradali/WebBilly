Aftercare

Keep them blooming

  1. Trim the stemsBefore arranging, cut the stems at an angle with a sharp knife or garden shears — it helps the flowers drink efficiently.
  2. Use clean waterFresh, cool water in the vase. Avoid hot water, as it can damage the flowers.
  3. Choose the right spotA cool, well-lit area, away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
  4. Change the waterEvery 2–3 days, to prevent bacteria and keep the flowers fresh.
  5. Remove wilted flowersTake out wilting blooms and dead outer petals to prolong the life of the bouquet.

Good to know

Questions

How far ahead should an order be placed?

At least 5 days before the date needed. For busy dates like Valentine’s and Mother’s Day, earlier is always better.

How does payment work?

Bouquets from the shop are paid in full at checkout. For bespoke designs, payment is arranged once the quote is agreed.

Where is delivery available?

Local delivery covers Birmingham and Solihull, subject to conditions. Collection is from B92, Solihull.

Can a design be changed?

Yes — size, colours, wrapping, ribbon and lettering can all be personalised. Inspiration pictures are always welcome.

What if a flower is out of season?

It’s swapped for a similar bloom in the same colours, so the bouquet always looks its freshest.

Are bridal and mehndi florals available?

Yes — gajre, wrist corsages, bridal bouquets and more. Send a bridal enquiry with the date and outfit colours for a personal quote.
