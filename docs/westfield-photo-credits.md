# Westfield Garage — stock photo credits

Every photograph on the Westfield Garage demo is licensed stock, downloaded to
`assets/westfield/` and served from our own origin — nothing is hot-linked. Each
is stored twice: `<name>.webp` at 1800px for the full-width slots and
`<name>-sm.webp` at 900px for phones and gallery thumbnails.

**None of these are photographs of his garage.** They are placeholders that make
the layout judgeable. Replacing them with real photographs of the workshop is
the single biggest improvement available to this page — drop the new files in
under the same names and no markup needs to change.

**Pexels licence** (pexels.com/license): free for commercial use, no attribution
required, modification allowed; cannot be resold as stock. Attribution is not
required, so none appears on the page — this table exists so we can prove
provenance and re-source a file later.

## Two notes on what is in frame

- **`svc-clutches`** shows a technician working under a raised car rather than a
  clutch plate on a bench. Free stock has almost no true clutch-disc photography
  ("clutch" on Pexels overwhelmingly returns handbags), and an underside shot is
  an honest picture of where a clutch job actually happens. If the garage sends
  a photo of a real clutch kit, swap it.
- **`svc-gearbox`** is a sectioned exhibition transmission, not a workshop bench
  shot. It reads unmistakably as a gearbox at tile size, which is what the slot
  needs.
