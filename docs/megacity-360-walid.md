# 360° tours on your phone — the whole job, start to finish

One page for Walid. Everything below happens on the phone, apart from printing
the QR code. Nothing is lost if you stop halfway: the tour saves itself.

## Before you leave the office

- [ ] The listing already exists in the Studio, with its rooms filled in
      (bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, garden). The tour builds itself from that,
      so the more the listing knows, the less you type.
- [ ] **iPhone only:** Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible**. On
      "High Efficiency" the phone saves HEIC photos, which browsers cannot read
      — you will get "HEIC photos can't be read here" when you try to add them.

## At the property

- [ ] Shoot one 360° photo per room, standing near the middle, with the lights
      on. Name nothing — you will say which room each one is in a moment.
- [ ] Sign in to the Studio on the phone (**/studio** on the agency's own
      domain; **billydigitals.com/templates/megacity-studio** until it moves),
      open the property, tap
      the **360** tab, then **Open the tour Studio**. It opens in the same tab;
      Back brings you here again.
- [ ] Tap the capture card ("Photograph the rooms") and choose the photos from
      the gallery. They are read one at a time — the card says "Reading photo 2
      of 7…" — so give it a moment on a big batch.
- [ ] For each one, answer **"Which room is this?"**. Rooms still without a 360
      are at the top of the list; there is also "A new room…" if you shot
      something the listing does not know about.
- [ ] If a 360 was already uploaded on the property's **Media** tab, it is
      offered right there — "Or use a 360° already uploaded to this listing" —
      and costs nothing to use.

## Doors between the rooms

- [ ] The doors are already linked from the listing: hallway to kitchen, landing
      to the bedrooms, en-suites off their bedroom. You only need to aim them.
- [ ] In the **Hotspots** tab, tap the spot in the photo where the doorway is;
      the door moves there and the way back is made for you.
- [ ] Walk the tour once yourself — tap each door and check you land where you
      expected.

## Saving

- [ ] There is no Save button. The chip near the top says **Saving…**, then
      **Saved**. "Not saved · Retry" means the phone lost signal — tap it once
      you have bars again.
- [ ] Don't close the tab while it says Saving.

## Publish

- [ ] Back on the **360** tab, look at the score ring. It has to reach the
      number the strip names (70 out of 100 unless the office changed it) and at
      least one room needs a 360.
- [ ] Tap **Publish tour**. If anything is missing, the tab lists it in plain
      words — fix it and publish again.
- [ ] "Published, but the listing is not live yet" means the tour is fine but
      the **property** is still a draft. Publish the listing and it appears.
- [ ] Changed something afterwards? The strip says **Changes not live** —
      tap **Publish latest changes**.

## Check it the way a tenant will

- [ ] Tap **Copy tour link for 10ninety**, send it to yourself on WhatsApp, and
      open it from there.
- [ ] Tap the phone icon in the toolbar ("Look around by moving your phone") and
      move the phone about — on an iPhone, allow Motion & Orientation when Safari
      asks. Tap it again to turn it off.
- [ ] Check **Plan** (the floor plan, with a marker for the room you are in),
      **Photos** (any extra photographs you added to that room) and **Details**.
- [ ] Tap **Book a viewing** and send yourself a test enquiry — it should land in
      the Studio's Enquiries inbox, marked as coming from the tour.
- [ ] Open the tour from the property page too (the frame partway down the
      page). Full screen from there shows a **← Back to property** bar at the
      top, so nobody gets stranded in the tour.

## The QR code

- [ ] On the 360 tab, **Phone preview and print** shows a QR code for the same
      link. Scan it to check the tour on another phone.
- [ ] **Download QR (PNG)** gives a print-ready image — the listing title above
      the code, the Megacity wordmark and the link below — for the window card
      and the brochure.

## Put it where tenants will find it

- [ ] Paste the link into the **virtual tour** box on the property in 10ninety,
      so the portals carry it.
- [ ] Before the website domain moves, the link starts `billydigitals.com` and
      the Studio says so. It works, but re-paste the new link into 10ninety
      after go-live.

## When the property is let

- [ ] **Take it off the listing** on the 360 tab. Visitors stop seeing it
      straight away; the tour itself stays in the Studio and can be published
      again for the next tenancy.
- [ ] Remove the link from 10ninety at the same time.

---

Two things that catch people out:

- **"This tour isn't published yet"** on a link you just sent means exactly
  that — the tour, or the property, is still a draft.
- **Start again** on the 360 tab throws the whole tour away and rebuilds it from
  the listing. The photos you uploaded on the Media tab are kept and offered
  back, but the doors you aimed are gone.
