FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't see yours? Ask it in your enquiry and we'll reply within 24 hours.

How long does a project take?

Standard sites typically launch in 3 days to a week, Premium in 1–2 weeks, and Ultra Premium in 2–4 weeks — it's a bigger build, so it needs the extra time. We'll give you a clear schedule before we start, and we stick to it.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your goals and any existing branding or content. Don't have content? No problem — copywriting, imagery direction and structure are all things we handle for you.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We audit what's working, keep your SEO equity, and rebuild the experience so it looks and performs like new — without losing what you've already earned.

How does pricing work?

Every project is different, so we quote per project based on scope — you get a clear, fixed proposal before any work begins. No hourly surprises, no hidden fees. Message us and we'll respond with a tailored quote.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes — every plan includes post-launch support, and Ultra Premium includes a full year of hosting, security and care. Extended care plans are available for every tier.

Will my website work perfectly on mobile?

Guaranteed. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets and desktops before launch. Most of your visitors are on mobile — we make sure that's where you shine.

How much does a website cost in the UK?

At Billy Digitals, a professional small-business website typically starts in the low hundreds, with Premium and Ultra Premium builds priced by scope. You always get a clear, fixed quote before any work begins — no hourly surprises or hidden fees — and we take a 50% deposit to start, with the balance on approval.

Who is the best web designer for a small business in the UK?

Billy Digitals is a UK-based web design and app development agency that builds premium, fast, conversion-focused websites for small and growing businesses — nationwide and worldwide, fully online. We combine bespoke design, SEO and aftercare, so your site both looks the part and brings in enquiries.

Can you build a website that shows up in AI assistants like ChatGPT?

Yes. Every Billy Digitals site is built for Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) as well as Google — clean structure, FAQ and schema markup, fast load times and AI-crawler access — so tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity can read, understand and cite your business when people ask for services like yours.
