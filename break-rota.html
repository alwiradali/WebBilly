Break Rota

12 people in 3 groups of 4. Four positions, each needing 2 people (8 at a time). Breaks go in waves: with 3 off at a time, one person from each group goes together, so each group always keeps 3 people working. Partners come from the same group: each group gives one pair, the three left over make one mixed pair and the spare. Everyone moves to a new spot every slot.
